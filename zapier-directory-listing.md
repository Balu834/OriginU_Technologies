## Company description

**Lead capture and follow-up automation for small businesses that can't afford to miss a customer.**

Most small businesses lose leads in the gaps between their tools: a form fill that never
reaches the CRM, a missed call nobody calls back, a reply that sits unread. OriginU builds
the Zapier workflows that close those gaps, so every lead is captured, routed and followed
up automatically.

We're a product studio first. We've built and run our own automation software in
production, so we design workflows that hold up under real volume, not demos that break
in a week.

## Services

- Lead capture → CRM → instant follow-up workflows (forms, calls, email, WhatsApp)
- Zap builds and fixes: multi-step Zaps, Paths, Filters, Formatter, Webhooks
- AI-powered steps: reply classification, lead qualification, drafting responses
- Custom integrations through Webhooks and APIs for apps without a native Zapier connector
- Zap audits: cut task usage, remove failure points, add error alerts
- Hand-off documentation and team training
